PocketFish support

Scan chess diagrams, analyse them with Stockfish, and train with Solve mode, rated puzzles and games against the engine — on your phone.

Contact

Questions, ideas, or a scan that went wrong? Write to PocketFish@AgileWithAI.com. If a position was misread, please attach the photo or screenshot: it helps make recognition better. In the app, Settings → Help & feedback opens a mail with the app version filled in.

The board wasn't found, or the frame is off

Drag the four corners onto the corners of the 8×8 squares (not the outer frame); the magnifier shows exactly where each corner sits. Photos work best when the board fills most of the picture, is roughly upright and has no strong glare.

A piece was misread

The review screen after a scan lets you fix it: drag pieces, or pick a piece from the palette and tap the square. Squares the app was unsure about are highlighted.

Whose move is it?

If a king is in check, the app knows whose move it is. Otherwise it uses your default (Settings → Scanning); switch it with the white and black king buttons under the board.

Analyze, Solve or Play?

Analyze shows the engine's evaluation, best lines and arrows. Solve hides them so you can find the move yourself: the engine then grades your move, gives hints or shows the solution. You choose after each scan, or with the switch at the top of the main screen. Play lets you play the position out against Stockfish at one of eight levels; you take the side to move, and Switch sides hands it to the engine.

How does the puzzle rating work?

Every puzzle counts once, like a game against the puzzle's own rating: solved at the first try raises your rating, a wrong move, a hint or the solution lowers it. A "?" after the rating means it is still settling (after your first few puzzles). The puzzles come from the lichess.org puzzle database (CC0) and are part of the app, so they work offline too.

Does it need the internet? Which devices?

No internet needed: recognition and the engine run on your device. PocketFish needs iOS 16.4 or newer (iPhone 8 / X and later).

Privacy and source code

PocketFish collects no data and never uploads your photos — see the privacy policy. To remove what the app keeps on your phone (settings, recent scans, Solve mode score, puzzle rating), use Settings → Privacy → Delete all data; recent scans can also be switched off there. PocketFish's source code is free software under the GNU GPL v3 and on GitHub (the trained recognition models are not part of it).
