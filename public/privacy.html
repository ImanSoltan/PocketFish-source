Privacy policy

PocketFish · last updated 3 November 2026

In short

PocketFish doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn't send anything anywhere: reading the photo and the chess engine both run on your device, which is why PocketFish also works without an internet connection. The one exception is a tip, if you choose to give one: Apple handles that payment (see Tips below). PocketFish is made by Dr. Iman Soltanzadeh (Agile With AI), who is responsible for it; the full address is in the legal notice.

Photos

Photos you take, choose or paste are processed on your device to recognise the chess position. They are never uploaded and are not saved to your photo library. While you scan, the app keeps a temporary copy in its private temporary folder, which the system clears automatically. PocketFish only sees the photos you take, pick or paste; it has no access to the rest of your photo library, and it only reads the clipboard when you tap Paste image.

What stays on your device

The app keeps your settings, the position you were last looking at, your Solve mode score, your puzzle rating and the puzzles you played, the side and engine level you last played with, and up to 24 recent scans (the position and a small picture of the board) in its own storage on your device. In Settings → Privacy you can turn off Remember recent scans, or remove everything with Delete all data; deleting the app removes everything too.

The chess engine (Stockfish) and the board recognition run entirely on your device, so PocketFish works without an internet connection.

Tips

Everything in PocketFish is free. In the iPhone app you can leave an optional tip under Settings → Help & feedback. Apple processes the payment through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy; PocketFish receives no name, email address or payment details. The app only remembers on your device that you gave a tip, to say thank you; Delete all data removes that too.

Links to other websites

If you tap lichess.org or chess.com, the current position is sent to that website as part of the link, and that website's privacy policy applies. The same goes for opening the source code on GitHub.

Feedback

If you send feedback, it goes out through your own mail app. Your message and email address are only used to reply to you. The prefilled mail includes the app version and your device's system version, which you can remove before sending.

Children

PocketFish collects no data from anyone, including children.
