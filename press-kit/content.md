## Short description (≈ 60 words)

PocketFish is the companion for every chess book: it turns any diagram into a position you can play with. Take
a photo of a book page or a screenshot, and the app recognises the board and every piece in seconds. Analyse it with Stockfish, find the
best move yourself in Solve mode, play it out against the engine, or train with thousands of rated puzzles.
Everything runs on your iPhone: nothing is uploaded.

## Longer description (≈ 150 words)

Chess books are full of positions worth exploring, but setting them up by hand is slow, and the book can't
tell you whether your own idea works. PocketFish reads a
diagram from a photo — printed books (old engraved ones too), magazines, or a screenshot — and opens it on an
interactive board in seconds. You can check and fix the recognised position in a tap.

From there, Stockfish, one of the strongest chess engines, analyses it right on the phone: evaluation, best
lines and arrows. Or hide the engine and use **Solve** mode: find the move yourself and get it graded, with
hints and the solution when you need them. **Play** the position out against Stockfish at eight levels, from
beginner to full strength. And keep sharp with thousands of **rated puzzles** and a puzzle of the day.

PocketFish is private by design: recognition and the engine run on the device, it works in flight mode, and
there's no account, no ads and no tracking. It speaks English, German, Spanish and French.

## Privacy (short block)

Your photos are read on your iPhone and never uploaded. The board recognition and the Stockfish engine run on
the device, so PocketFish works without an internet connection. No account, no ads, no analytics, no
tracking.

## Notes for the website

- Say "in beta" / "testing" until the App Store listing is live; Apple's "Download on the App Store" badge
  may only be used once the app is available there.
- Mention Stockfish as the engine the app uses, without suggesting that the Stockfish project endorses
  PocketFish. Don't use the lichess.org or chess.com logos.
- The App Store and TestFlight need **public** privacy policy and support pages. Their texts are in this
  repository (`public/privacy.html`, `public/support.html`); hosting them on the website (e.g.
  `agilewithai.com/pocketfish/privacy` and `/support`) is the simplest way while the repository is private.
